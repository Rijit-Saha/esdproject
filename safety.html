Safety Instructions for E-Bike Riders

  • Always wear a certified helmet while riding.
  • Follow local traffic rules and signs.
  • Ride in bike lanes where available.
  • Use hand signals before turning or stopping.
  • Do not ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Do not carry passengers on single-rider bikes.
  • Check brakes, tires, and battery before every ride.
  • Park bikes at designated docking stations after use.

User Information & Guidelines

  • Create an account with a valid phone number and email.
  • Maintain updated personal details in your profile.
  • Respect time limits on bookings; extra charges may apply.
  • Report any damage or issues via the support tab in the app.
  • Use the app to check available bikes and dock locations.
  • Recharge your wallet for a seamless booking experience.
  • Violations may result in account suspension.
